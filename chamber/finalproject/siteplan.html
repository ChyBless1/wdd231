ELMAVID Website Site Plan

Site Name

ELMAVID

ELMAVID is a family-inspired brand name created from parts of the names of the owner's four children. “EL” comes from Gabriela, “MA” comes from Maravilha, “VI” comes from David, and the final “D” represents Chidera, also called Dera.

The name was selected because it is personal, memorable, easy to pronounce, and suitable for a premium brand offering insulated food flasks and vacuum water bottles.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the ELMAVID website is to introduce a people-first brand that values health, quality, safety, and everyday well-being. ELMAVID will offer premium insulated food flasks and vacuum water bottles designed to keep meals and drinks hot or cold while supporting safer and more responsible food and drink storage.

The brand aims to help customers make informed purchasing decisions by presenting clear information about each product's material, capacity, features, intended use, and care instructions. ELMAVID prioritises durable, food-safe products that can help reduce reliance on unsuitable or disposable plastic containers.

The website will allow customers to explore and compare products, view detailed product information, select items that meet their needs, and purchase or submit an enquiry about the products they are interested in.

Scenarios

The following questions represent the type of information that ELMAVID customers may look for when visiting the website:

  • What food flask and vacuum bottle capacities are available, and can I find sizes ranging from 500 ml to 2 litres?
  • Is each product made from 304 or 316 stainless steel, and where can I find its material specification?
  • How long will each product keep food or drinks hot or cold, and which models provide approximately 8-hour or up to 12-hour temperature retention?
  • How can I compare the available sizes, materials and features before purchasing the product that best suits my needs?

Color Scheme

ELMAVID will use a distinctive and colourful palette that reflects warmth, wellness, confidence and premium quality. The colours will create a lively brand identity without using the common blue-and-gold combination.

  • Pomegranate Red — #8F1D3F: This will be the primary brand colour. It will be used for the header, footer, major headings, navigation and primary call-to-action buttons.

  • Warm Coral — #E76F51: This will be used for borders, product badges, highlights, active states and selected decorative elements.

  • Eucalyptus Green — #1F5A55: This will represent health, quality and responsible product choices. It will be used for secondary headings, icons and secondary buttons.

  • Warm Cream — #FFF4E8: This will be the main page background and will provide a soft, welcoming surface for the website content.

  • Deep Charcoal — #2B2326: This will be used for paragraphs and other important text because it is softer than pure black while remaining clear and readable.

Typography

DM Serif Display

DM Serif Display will be used for the ELMAVID brand name, page headings, section headings, and selected promotional text. Its elegant appearance will support the premium and professional identity of the brand.

Manrope

Manrope will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, product information, form labels, and other general website content. It was selected because it is modern, clear, and easy to read on both large and small screens.
